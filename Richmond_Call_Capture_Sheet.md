## The seven planned questions

| \# | Question | Why it matters (what it decides) | Answer |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Q6 | **Capacity.** How tightly is the entering class held? Is it a hard seat cap or a soft target? What band (for example ±3%, ±5%, ±10%) would they accept? | Decides the price-test verdict. At ±3% a test is Not recommended; at ±10% it can be Optional or Recommend. Also sets the class-size constraint in the optimizer | They will hit their numbers. Hard seat cap. They will fill their class to 100% |
| Q2 | **Existing price evidence.** Is there a published or index-based scholarship grid (LSAT/UGPA thresholds with award levels)? Has the award schedule changed in the last five cycles (what, when, which students)? | Decides whether any anchor exists without a test. No threshold and no policy change means no anchor, and FAO runs in the weak-evidence state | Yes, but I don’t have it now. Yes, the award schedule is changing. Previously, they awarded full tuition scholarships to the top quartile of admits, and the bottom quartile paid full tuition (\$0 scholarship). Last year (2025 admit cycle), that was 9 full rides and 9 full tuition paying students. The rest were in the middle. Richmond wants to move away from that model and distribute aid more evenly across their class and its quartiles. They want to use bonus aid to target geographies either by state or by MSA. So, we won’t need ZIP5; indeed, ZIP3 is going to work fine for us. They will be holding the discount rate static this year. They are proposing a tuition rate migration of just above 5% (up from 2.5-3.8% per year in the recent past) |
| Q8 | **Blinding (new).** Can aid staff deliver a test bonus as a formula line with no flag for test membership? Who sees award details? | Decides whether the all-eligible analysis is usable as the primary estimate. | Q1 | **Objective.** Net tuition revenue, class size, median LSAT/UGPA position, or a mix? If position, which target and why (rankings, board)? | Sets the objective and targets. Listen for how firm the target is | They have net tuition revenue goals that they need to hit. | Q3 | **Need.** Are FAFSA or institutional need data used in scholarship decisions at all? | Decides whether need is a grid dimension in the law profile | yes |
| Q4 | **Renewal rule.** Are awards fixed-dollar or a percentage of tuition at renewal? Any renewal conditions (GPA, rank, credits)? Not discussed. |
| Q5 | **Randomization appetite.** Would they accept a bonus-aid test? A one-year bonus instead of renewable? A timing or deadline test?
