## Ask these first

The audit showed that the price-test verdict turns on the capacity band (Q6), not the objective (Q1). Q8 decides whether the best test analysis is usable. If time runs short, get Q6, Q2, Q8 and Q1 in that order.

## The seven planned questions

| # | Question | Why it matters (what it decides) | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q6 | **Capacity.** How tightly is the entering class held? Is it a hard seat cap or a soft target? What band (for example ±3%, ±5%, ±10%) would they accept? | Decides the price-test verdict. At ±3% a test is Not recommended; at ±10% it can be Optional or Recommend. Also sets the class-size constraint in the optimizer | |
| Q2 | **Existing price evidence.** Is there a published or index-based scholarship grid (LSAT/UGPA thresholds with award levels)? Has the award schedule changed in the last five cycles (what, when, which students)? | Decides whether any anchor exists without a test. No threshold and no policy change means no anchor, and FAO runs in the weak-evidence state | |
| Q8 | **Blinding (new).** Can aid staff deliver a test bonus as a formula line with no flag for test membership? Who sees award details? | Decides whether the all-eligible analysis is usable as the primary estimate. If staff cannot be blind, the primary estimate is test-only | |
| Q1 | **Objective.** Net tuition revenue, class size, median LSAT/UGPA position, or a mix? If position, which target and why (rankings, board)? | Sets the objective and targets. Note: the audit found that at Richmond's guardrail, aid cannot move the enrolled median, so a position target is likely infeasible for aid alone. Listen for how firm the target is | |
| Q3 | **Need.** Are FAFSA or institutional need data used in scholarship decisions at all? | Decides whether need is a grid dimension in the law profile | |
| Q4 | **Renewal rule.** Are awards fixed-dollar or a percentage of tuition at renewal? Any renewal conditions (GPA, rank, credits)? Do they plan tuition increases (about +3.9% a year recently)? | Sets the three-year revenue model and the horizon. Richmond reports no conditional scholarships in 509; confirm | |
| Q5 | **Randomization appetite.** Would they accept a bonus-aid test? A one-year bonus instead of renewable? A timing or deadline test? Who would have to approve? | Decides which evidence route is available. Also the alignment-gate disclosure | |
| Q7 | **Data readiness.** Three to five cohorts of admits: offer dates, every package version, LSAT and UGPA, engagement, deposit and census status. System of record (Slate, Banner, other)? Who owns the extract? | Decides Tier 1 versus Tier 2 and the timeline | |
