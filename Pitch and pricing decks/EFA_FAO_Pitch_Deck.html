Financial Aid Optimization
We measure how students respond to price
The foundation of every aid recommendation

The question that matters most is what an extra dollar of aid does to a student’s decision to enroll.

Randomized test

Our first test: bonus-aid

A random share of eligible admits receive extra aid ($0, $2,000, or $4,000). Nobody receives less than they otherwise would. This test gives us a clean answer to “what does a dollar do?”

Award-rule analysis

Thresholds to guide award rules

Where your merit grid steps up at a GPA or test cutoff, students just above and just below are comparable. We use a threshold test wherever it checks out, in order to refine the award rules.

›
Combined
A single, anchored price response
The output of tests from each source is reported on its own, then combined. We flag when indicators disagree or point in different directions.
›
Used for

One transparent model for every segment

Clear directions emerge because we apply the same standards to every prospect, so each recommendation can be explained and defended. Any adjustments you make post-hoc also have a standard reference for the decision.

Start with what you haveTier 1 uses your award rules alone, with a wider range.
Strengthen with a testTier 2 adds the bonus-aid test, approved by your enrollment and financial aid team.
Sharper every cycleA standing test narrows the estimate each year.

Responsible by design
Built to be trusted by your leadership and your campus
Aid decisions carry weight for brand equity and reputation. Frankel safeguards all the important decisions.

Fair by design

Awards follow policy, not PII.
  • Race, ethnicity, and gender are never model inputs; they are used only to audit results
  • Policy groups use academic, need, and residency attributes only
  • No consumer credit data
  • An equity audit accompanies every recommendation

Private and compliant by design

Student records stay in a secure, FERPA-approved environment.
  • De-identified data with hashed keys; no names, contact details, or full ZIP codes
  • The dashboard shows aggregates only
  • Groups under 10 students are suppressed by default; each client sets its own policy for named, authenticated staff
  • Raw files deleted at engagement close

Honest, everywhere

Numbers you can explain and defend.
  • No number appears without its range or a stated reason it has none
  • Only information known at the decision date enters the models
  • Missing data is never treated as zero

Reviewed, refined, and approved by your team

Nothing is applied until you say so.
  • You and your leadership review every recommendation before implementation
  • Each model release is tested on synthetic data where the right answer is known
  • Each engagement includes a backtest on your own most recent cohort
